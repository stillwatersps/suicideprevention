What happens to what you share here

In this app, what you choose and write is saved on this phone only, so it's here next time. Tidelight never receives it, reads it, or analyzes it.

If this phone's iCloud or Google backup is on, the backup includes it. Delete everything, in Your journey, removes it from the phone now. A copy in an earlier backup can last until the phone's next backup.

Anyone who can unlock this phone can open the app. Quick exit leaves fast. Nobody at Tidelight sees what you write, so it isn't a way to ask for help. In a crisis, call or text 988, or call 911.
