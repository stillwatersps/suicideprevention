About Stillwater

Stillwater is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. Stillwater is not a crisis service. The calling, texting, and chat options on this site connect you to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
