You can reach out yourself

You can call or text 988 yourself, even if there is no adult you can tell. Its rules don't set a minimum age, and you don't have to give your name or age to get support. It can help to save 988 in your phone now, in case you need it later.

  • The Trevor Project

    For LGBTQ+ young people. Its terms describe the phone lifeline as for people under age 25. Trained counselors by phone, text, or chat, free and confidential, 24/7. Message and data rates may apply to texts.

    Call 1-866-488-7386 Text START to 678-678

  • LGBTQ+ youth, through 988

    The 988 Lifeline says its specialized service for LGBTQ+ youth has been restored. It is separate from The Trevor Project.

    Call 988, then press 3 Text PRIDE to 988

  • Crisis Text Line

    Trained volunteer crisis counselors by text, free and confidential, 24/7, anywhere in the United States. Crisis Text Line says your first message can say anything, and a word like HOME helps it know how you found it. Its FAQ says messages are free on AT&T, T-Mobile, Sprint, and Verizon. On other carriers, it says standard rates apply and 741741 will appear on the bill. Its terms say message and data rates may apply. Crisis Text Line says many T-Mobile prepaid plans don't allow numbers like 741741. It also says it isn't available yet on Google Voice or TextNow. If a text doesn't go through, it offers web chat and WhatsApp on its website. You can also call or text 988.

    Text HOME to 741741

More options, including Spanish, ASL, and other languages, are on Get Help.

Telling an adult you trust

It can also help to tell an adult you trust, such as a parent or guardian, a relative, a teacher, a coach, a spiritual leader, or a doctor. It's okay to ask what they can keep private first.

If you tell a school counselor you are thinking about suicide, expect them to tell a parent or guardian. Their national association treats this as required, even for students who are 18. If a parent's abuse or neglect is the reason, the counselor contacts child protective services instead.

What stays private

Crisis lines keep conversations confidential, with limits. The 988 Lifeline says it may share information with 911, police, or a mobile crisis team, without your permission, in some situations. For example, if someone is in danger of being hurt very soon, or if a young person is being abused or neglected under state law. The Trevor Project and Crisis Text Line have similar limits. The Trevor Project says it won't tell parents or guardians unless the law or a subpoena requires it.

A small number of 988 centers bill insurance and may ask for your details. You can say no and still get support. If you give them and a claim is filed, a parent or guardian could get an insurance statement.

On a shared phone or computer, visits to this site can show up in browser history, and calls or texts can show up in phone logs. The Quick exit button hides this page at once and opens weather.gov, but it can't clear your history. See what it can and can't hide.

If you're worried about a friend

You don't need to have the answers. Start with a caring question and listen. If you're worried about suicide, it's okay to ask directly: "Are you thinking about suicide?" Encourage your friend to talk to a trusted adult, such as a parent, coach, teacher, or school counselor, and talk to one yourself.

If your friend talks about suicide or about hurting themselves, don't keep it a secret, even if they ask you to. Tell a trusted adult, or call or text 988 to ask what to do. You can contact 988 at any time, even if it's about a friend.

If they say they are going to end their life, call 911 if it's an emergency. If you're with them and it is safe, stay with them. If they told you by text or online, keep talking if you can while you get an adult, or call or text 988. You don't have to handle this by yourself.

About Tidelight

Tidelight is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. Tidelight is not a crisis service. The call, text, and chat links on this site connect you to outside services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

Privacy: this site has no sign-ups, forms, analytics, or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security. Quick exit and saved offline copies have limits. Read about privacy and what this site can't hide.
