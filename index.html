Suicide prevention & crisis support

You are not alone.

If you're feeling overwhelmed, in crisis, or having thoughts of suicide, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is there for you — anytime, day or night.

  • Call 988Talk to someone now
  • Text 988Reach out by text
  • Chat Online988 Lifeline web chat

On a device that can't call or text? Dial or text 988 from any phone, or go to 988lifeline.org. These connect you to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, not to Stillwater.

About Stillwater

Stillwater is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. Stillwater is not a crisis service. The calling, texting, and chat options above connect you to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
