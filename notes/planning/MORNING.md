## What to check before calling a prototype ready

- Each support action and navigation item has an explicit, tested destination.
- Phone text and controls remain usable without horizontal scrolling.
- Keyboard and screen-reader paths are usable.
- Content sources, availability claims, and review status are recorded.
- The website describes the service it actually provides.

Detailed checks and primary sources are in the two companion notes. No clinical
review, app implementation, or release validation has been completed by this draft.
