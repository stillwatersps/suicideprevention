You don't have to have everything figured out.

For your mind

  • Take a few slow breaths and notice where you are: what you can see, hear, and feel.
  • Write down one thing that's on your mind. You don't have to solve it.
  • Take a short break from what you're doing, even for five minutes.
  • Let yourself rest without having to earn it first.
  • Notice one thing that went okay today, however small.
  • Step outside or open a window for a few minutes.

For your connection

  • Send a short message to someone you feel comfortable with. It can be as simple as "thinking of you."
  • Talk with someone you trust about how today has been.
  • If you'd like to talk with someone right now, you can call or text 988 at any time.
  • Spend a little time near other people, even without talking, such as at a library or a park.
  • Tell someone about something good from today.

For yourself

  • Revisit something you used to enjoy, even for ten minutes.
  • Listen to a song that has meant something to you.
  • Do one small thing just for you, with no goal attached.
  • Try something small and new, like a different walk or a new way to cook something you already have.
  • Write down one thing you appreciate about yourself.
  • Spend some time outside, at whatever pace feels right.

Finding your way back

Sometimes, when life gets difficult, we lose touch with the things that make us feel like ourselves. You don't need to rediscover everything at once. Even the smallest step matters.

Kept in Your journey, for this visit. You can keep editing it here.

Rediscover something

Choose anything you're drawn to, and get one low-pressure idea. None of them cost money.

See every idea
  • Take a short walk somewhere you haven't been in a while.
  • Sit outside for a few minutes and notice the sky.
  • Listen to an album you loved at a different time in your life.
  • Make a short playlist of songs that feel like you.
  • Pick up an old hobby for ten minutes, without needing to finish anything.
  • Draw, write, or build something small, just to see what happens.
  • Stretch or move in a way that feels good to you.
  • Dance to one song, alone or with someone.
  • Look up something you've always been curious about.
  • Borrow a library book about something new to you.
  • Reconnect with a friend you've missed. A short message is enough.
  • Plan a small, free thing to do with someone you trust.
  • Read a few pages of something you enjoy.

            Support

            Tidelight is not a crisis service, and it doesn't replace professional care. If things feel like too much, you don't have to wait until they get worse to reach out.

            • Talking with someone you trust, such as a friend, family member, teacher, or doctor, can help. You don't need to have the right words.
            • For support over time, Get Help lists ways to find care.
            • Tidelight lists services in the United States only. Outside the United States, contact your local emergency number.

            Need to talk to someone now?

            • Call 988Talk to a counselor
            • Text 988Send any message
            • Chat OnlineWeb chat with 988

            988 says a text starts with a few steps. Reply NEXT to accept its terms, answer a brief survey, and wait for a counselor.

            The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is free, confidential, and available any time. It is a separate service from Tidelight.

            On a device that can't call or text? Call or text 988 from a phone, or go to 988lifeline.org. In immediate danger, call 911.

            About Tidelight

            Tidelight is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. Tidelight is not a crisis service. The call, text, and chat links on this site connect you to outside services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

            Privacy: this site has no sign-ups, forms, analytics, or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security. Quick exit and saved offline copies have limits. Read about privacy and what this site can't hide.
