What Tidelight is

Tidelight is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. It is a website with information and links.

Tidelight is not a crisis service or medical care. Nobody at Tidelight watches this site or can see who is using it, and there is no way to send a message through it. For help, use the call, text, and chat options at the top of each page. They connect you to outside services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

An AI assistant drafted the text on this site for the two people starting this project. Tidelight has not been tested as a way to prevent suicide.

Your privacy

  • What this site collects. It has no sign-ups, forms, analytics, ads, or cookies, and it doesn't load anything from other websites.
  • The web host. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub.
  • Other services. When you call, text, chat, or open a link to another website, that service's own rules apply. See what happens when you reach out to 988.
  • On a shared phone or computer. Visits to this site can show up in browser history, and calls or texts can show up in phone logs.

What Quick exit can and can't do

The Quick exit button opens the weather.gov home page in the same tab. It also replaces the page you were on in that tab's history, so Back won't return to that page.

It can't:

  • Remove other Tidelight pages you opened earlier in that tab. Back can still reach them.
  • Clear your browser's history, other tabs, or the saved copies described below.
  • Remove a contact you saved, or calls and texts from your phone's logs.

If JavaScript is turned off, Quick exit is an ordinary link, so Back can return to the page you left. With no connection, weather.gov can't load, and your browser shows its own error page instead.

A private or incognito window can keep pages out of this device's history. To remove pages you've already visited, clear your history and website data in your browser's settings.

Saved copies and using this site offline

On a secure connection, your browser may save a copy of this site's pages and files after your first visit, so the crisis numbers can still show with a weak signal or none.

  • What works offline. After that first visit, the pages of this site can open with no connection, in English and Spanish. An address that wasn't saved shows the "page not found" page, which has the crisis numbers.
  • What doesn't. Calling and texting need phone service. Chat and links to other websites need an internet connection.
  • How updates arrive. With a connection, the site tries for the newest version first, which can take up to about 10 minutes to appear. A saved copy is used only when the newest version can't load, so it could be out of date. When your device reports no connection, the page says so. If a number doesn't work, try 911 or another option on this page.
  • Don't rely on it alone. Browsers can delete saved copies, for example when a device is low on storage or you haven't used the site for a while. Keeping 988 in your contacts or on a wallet card doesn't depend on this.
  • Your privacy. Saved copies stay on your device. Nothing is sent to Tidelight, but on a shared device they are a trace of your visit, and Quick exit can't remove them. Clearing website data in your browser's settings does.

Saving 988 to your contacts

The "Save 988 to your contacts" button opens a small contact card file with the 988 Lifeline's name, number, and website. It comes from this site, and nothing about you is sent to Tidelight. You choose whether to save it. On a shared phone, or if your contacts sync to another device, other people may see it.

About Tidelight

Tidelight is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. Tidelight is not a crisis service. The call, text, and chat links on this site connect you to outside services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
