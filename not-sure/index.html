A gentle place to start

Not sure what you're feeling? Start here.

You don't need to have the right words. Take a moment to notice what is on your mind. You can explore your feelings at your own pace, without pressure or judgment. If you need support, there are people ready to listen.

If you're thinking about suicide

Even if you aren't sure, you can call 988 or text 988 now. You don't need to be in an emergency or know what to say. SAMHSA, a U.S. government health agency, says there is no reason too big or too small to reach out. In immediate danger, call 911.

Before you start

  • This isn't a test. There are no scores and no right answers.
  • There is nothing to type. What you open here isn't saved or sent.
  • You can skip any part, stop at any time, or go straight to talking with someone.

What feeling is strongest right now?

Open whichever one fits, more than one, or none at all.

Overwhelmed

Everyone feels stress from time to time, and the National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) says it can sometimes feel overwhelming. You don't have to work it all out right now.

NIMH suggests talking to people you trust about how you are feeling. If you can't manage the stress, or it won't let up, a health care provider can help too.

Sad

NIMH says everyone feels sad or low sometimes, and these feelings usually pass.

If a low mood doesn't go away, or you think it could be depression, NIMH suggests talking to a health care provider. This page can't tell you whether it's depression. A provider can.

Anxious

NIMH says occasional anxiety is a normal part of life. Many people worry about things like health, money, school, work, or family.

If it doesn't go away and starts to get in the way of your daily activities, NIMH suggests talking to a health care provider.

Numb or empty

Struggling doesn't always feel like sadness. NIMH lists a lasting "empty" mood among the signs of depression. It says some older adults notice a lack of emotions instead of a low mood. SAMHSA's page for young people lists feeling numb or like nothing matters among reasons to talk to a trusted adult.

If you feel empty or hopeless, or feel you have no reason to live, please reach out now. NIMH lists these feelings among the warning signs of suicide. You can call 988 or text 988 any time.

Unsure, or something else

That's okay. You don't need a name for what you feel to talk with someone you trust or to contact 988. SAMHSA says there is no reason too big or too small to reach out.

What would you like to do next?

  • Speak with someone. Call 988, text 988, or reach out to someone you trust.
  • See your options. Get Help lists other services and what to expect from them.
  • If you've lost someone to suicide, After a loss may be a better place to start.
  • If you're a teen, For Teens covers reaching out yourself and who might be told.
  • Take a break. You can stop here and come back any time. This page keeps no record of what you open. Your browser may remember the visit, and the web host logs visitors' IP addresses. What this site can't hide.

When to talk to a professional

NIMH suggests seeking professional help if you have severe or distressing symptoms that have lasted 2 weeks or more. A primary care provider can refer you to a mental health professional. NIMH has tips for that conversation, and SAMHSA's FindTreatment.gov can help you search for care near you.

You don't have to wait 2 weeks to reach out. You can call or text 988 at any point, and call 911 in an emergency.

About Tidelight

Tidelight is an early project to help people in the United States find suicide prevention support. Tidelight is not a crisis service. The call, text, and chat links on this site connect you to outside services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

Privacy: this site has no sign-ups, forms, analytics, or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security. Quick exit and saved offline copies have limits. Read about privacy and what this site can't hide.
